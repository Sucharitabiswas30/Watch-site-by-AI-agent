# 01 — Header and hero

## Purpose
Establish Aevum as a quiet, modern watch house and lead visitors into the newest collection.

## Content
- Narrow delivery/returns announcement
- Transparent three-column navigation
- Centered wordmark
- Full-height dark watch campaign image
- Collection eyebrow, oversized serif headline, editorial copy, and text CTA

## Responsive behavior
Desktop navigation condenses to Menu, wordmark, and Bag on small screens. Hero copy remains bottom-aligned and image focus shifts right.
