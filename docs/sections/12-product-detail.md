# 12 — Product details and quick view

Every catalogue image and product title opens a dedicated `/products/[id]` page with a same-product slider offering full, dial, case, and strap views through controlled crops of the product's own photograph. The page also includes wearer and collection information, case and movement specifications, pricing, demo add-to-bag feedback, service details, an editorial brand panel, related watches, and the shared site footer.

The collection grid also provides an accessible quick-view modal with essential specifications, price, add-to-bag, and a direct link to the full product page. Escape or clicking outside closes the modal.

All add-to-bag actions use the same persistent cart drawer, including quantity adjustment, removal, subtotal calculation, and demo checkout.
