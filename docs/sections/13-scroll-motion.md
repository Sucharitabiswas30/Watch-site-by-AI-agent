# 13 — GSAP scroll motion

A shared GSAP ScrollTrigger layer adds varied scroll-based motion across the homepage, collection catalogue, and product-detail routes. Campaign copy slides laterally, headings skew upward, story text scales softly, wearer cards enter from opposing sides, collection products use a restrained scale-and-rotation reveal, and related products slide horizontally. Image surfaces use different directional wipes by section, while selected photography and background panels retain controlled parallax.

The motion layer automatically disables itself when the visitor prefers reduced motion.
