# Aevum storefront section plan

The homepage uses the pacing of the Timex India storefront—large editorial moments alternating with focused shopping modules—while all branding, writing, products, prices, and imagery are original demo content.

1. `01-header-hero.md` — announcement, navigation, launch story
2. `02-shop-by-wearer.md` — men/women editorial split
3. `03-feature-story.md` — full-width campaign and product rail
4. `04-new-arrivals.md` — demo product cards and cart controls
5. `05-popular-categories.md` — visual collection shortcuts
6. `06-maker-spotlight.md` — craftsmanship feature
7. `07-editorial-panels.md` — two immersive product stories
8. `08-more-to-explore.md` — supporting category cards
9. `09-journal-newsletter.md` — reading strip and email signup
10. `10-footer.md` — service, navigation, legal, social
11. `11-collection-page.md` — searchable 60-product catalogue
12. `12-product-detail.md` — product detail route and collection quick view
13. `13-scroll-motion.md` — shared GSAP ScrollTrigger animation layer
